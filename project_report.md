# **Customer Behavior Analysis**

### **Retail Sales, Customer Behaviour & Satisfaction**

## **Background and Overview**
The dataset represents a hypothetical retail clothing company that sells a variety of products across four main categories: Clothing, Footwear, Accessories, and Outerwear. The dataset was obtained from Kaggle under the name Customer Behavior and Purchase Dataset. It contains approximately 100 000 records and 21 original variables. The company serves customers across the USA and offers a subscription service, promotions, and different payment methods. 
Like many retail businesses, the company must understand its sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour to make informed business decisions. Identifying which product categories generate the most revenue, which customer groups contribute the most to sales, and how customer satisfaction varies across products can help the business identify opportunities for improvement and growth.
This analysis uses the available sales and customer data to evaluate business performance and identify patterns that may support better sales strategies and customer engagement.

## **Executive Summary**
Over the three-year period, the company generated approximately $5.3 million USD in total sales revenue, with more than 100,000 customer records across the country. Overall, the results indicate strong sales performance, with several notable trends in revenue, customer churn, and customer satisfaction.

- Sales Performance: 2024 was the strongest year, generating approximately $2 million in revenue from around 41,000 customer records. This represented approximately 40% of total revenue. Sales were particularly strong in the fourth quarter of 2024.
- Customer Churn: Approximately 92% of records were classified as not churned, while 8% were classified as churned.
- Customer Satisfaction: The average customer review rating was 3.85 out of 5, suggesting generally positive customer feedback.

The analysis identifies an opportunity to improve customer satisfaction within the clothing category, particularly for sweaters, which generated the highest sales but received the lowest average review rating. Addressing potential product quality or customer experience concerns could help the company maintain strong sales while improving customer satisfaction.
