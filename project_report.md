# **Customer Behavior Analysis**

### **Retail Sales, Customer Behaviour & Satisfaction**

## **Background and Overview**
The dataset represents a hypothetical retail clothing company that sells a variety of products across four main categories: Clothing, Footwear, Accessories, and Outerwear. The dataset was obtained from Kaggle under the name Customer Behavior and Purchase Dataset. It contains approximately 100 000 records and 21 original variables. The company serves customers across the USA and offers a subscription service, promotions, and different payment methods. 
Like many retail businesses, the company must understand its sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour to make informed business decisions. Identifying which product categories generate the most revenue, which customer groups contribute the most to sales, and how customer satisfaction varies across products can help the business identify opportunities for improvement and growth.
This analysis uses the available sales and customer data to evaluate business performance and identify patterns that may support better sales strategies and customer engagement.

## **Executive Summary**
Over the three-year period, the company generated approximately $5.3 million USD in total sales revenue, with more than 100,000 customer records across the country. Overall, the results indicate strong sales performance, with several notable trends in revenue, customer churn, and customer satisfaction.

- Sales Performance: 2024 was the strongest year, generating approximately $2 million in revenue from around 41,000 customer records. This represented approximately 40% of total revenue. Sales were particularly strong in the fourth quarter of 2024.
- Customer Churn: Approximately 92% of records were classified as not churned, while 8% were classified as churned.
- Customer Satisfaction: The average customer review rating was 3.85 out of 5, suggesting generally positive customer feedback.

The analysis identifies an opportunity to improve customer satisfaction within the clothing category, particularly for sweaters, which generated the highest sales but received the lowest average review rating. Addressing potential product quality or customer experience concerns could help the company maintain strong sales while improving customer satisfaction.

The company's sales performance showed consistent growth over the three-year period from January 2022 to December 2024. Total revenue increased by approximately 19% in 2023 and a further 25% in 2024, indicating positive year-over-year revenue growth. This suggests that the business strengthened its sales performance over the period, although further analysis would be needed to determine the factors driving this growth.

### **Seasonal Sales Trends**
- **Findings:** Sales performance varied across the four quarters, with Q4 consistently  generating the highest revenue and Q1 recording the lowest. On average, Q4 generated around $535,000 per year, while Q1 averaged approximately $353,000. This represents a difference of approximately 34%.
- **Action:** Introducing post-holiday promotions, seasonal discounts, and targeted marketing campaigns during Q1. Focusing on products that could maintain demand outside the holiday season.
- **Expected Impact:** Reduce the difference between Q1 and Q4 revenue and improve sales consistency throughout the year.
- **Metric to Track:** Q1 revenue, Q1 year over year growth, average order value, promotional campaign conversion rate.

### **Diversifying Revenue**
- **Findings:** Clothing contributes approximately 67% to the total revenue with sweaters alone making up 43% of the revenue. Footwear is the second biggest category making up 16.8% of the total revenue. 
- **Action:** Explore opportunities to promote other categories such as footwear, accessories, and outerwear through product bundles or targeted promotions. Explore states where other categories perform well and create marketing campaigns targeted to those areas. 
- **Expected Impact:** Increase revenue from currently smaller categories and reduce reliance on clothing and sweaters in particular.
- **Metrics to Track:** Revenue by category, category revenue growth, average purchase amount by category.

### **Geographic Growth Opportunities**
- **Findings:** Alabama generated the highest state-level revenue at approximately $132K, representing 2.5% of the total revenue. 
- **Action:** Compare customer counts, average purchase amounts, and category preference across states. Test targeted marketing campaigns in states with promising customer demand but lower revenue.
- **Expected Impact:** Identify underperforming markets with potential for growth and improve geographic sales distribution.
- **Metrics to Track:** Revenue by state, revenue growth by state, customer count by state, average purchase amount by state. 

## **Customer Behaviour and Retention**

Churn rate is the percentage of customers who stop doing business with a company over a given period. The dataset indicates that approximately 92% of customers were classified as not churned, while only 8% were classified as churned. This suggests a relatively low proportion of customers were flagged as churned within the dataset. However, the higher churn rate observed among young adults presents an opportunity for the company to investigate which customer segments may require additional engagement.

### **Improving Engagement** 
- **Findings:** Young adults represent the third largest customer segment with 22,560 records, and have the highest churn rate among the age groups at approximately 12%. Young adult subscribers spend an average of $51.68. 
- **Action:** Develop targeted engagement strategies for younger customers, such as personalized product recommendations, exclusive offers, and email campaigns. 
- **Expected Impact:** Encourage continued purchasing activity, reduce churn among younger customers, and build stronger relationships with this customer segment. 
- **Metrics to Track:** Young adult churn rate, repeat purchase rate, average purchase amount, campaign engagement rate.

### **Evaluate Fffectiveness of the Subscription Program**
- **Findings:** Subscribers and non-subscribers have approximately the same average purchase amount and subscribers have a slightly higher churn rate.
- **Action:** Review the benefits offered through the subscription program and gather customer feedback to understand whether the benefits encourage continued engagement. Consider introducing benefits that provide more value to subscribers.
- **Expected Impact:** Improve subscriber satisfaction, increase the perceived value of the program, and encourage continued participation.
- **Metrics to Track:** Subscriber churn rate, subscription cancellation rate, average purchase amount by subscription status, subscriber engagement.

### **Explore Opportunities Within Underrepresented Customer Segments**
- **Findings:** Approximately 70% of customers are male, while female customers have a slightly higher average purchase amount.
- **Action:** Investigate the purchasing preferences and product interests of female customers. Develop targeted marketing campaigns and product recommendations to better engage this segment.
- **Expected Impact:** Identify opportunities to increase revenue from an existing customer segment and potentially expand the customer base.
- **Metrics to Track:** Revenue by gender, average purchase amount by gender, customer count by gender, campaign conversion rate.


## **Customer Value and Satisfaction**

The analysis revealed differences in both sales performance and customer satisfaction across product categories. Accessories received the highest average review rating, despite contributing the least to total revenue. Within this category, belts received the highest average product rating of 3.93 out of 5. Clothing had the second-highest average category rating while remaining the company's largest revenue-generating category. 

### **Investigating Customer Dissatisfaction with Products** 
- **Findings:** Sweaters generate the most revenue but have 9,221 low ratings, representing approximately 21% of sweater reviews. Sandals have the highest proportion of low ratings at approximately 23%.
- **Action:** Examine customer reviews to identify recurring complaints. Investigate potential issues with sizing, materials, product quality, comfort, and product descriptions. Prioritize sandals for further investigation based on their higher proportion of low ratings, while also reviewing sweaters due to their substantial sales volume.
- **Expected Impact:** Improve customer satisfaction, reduce negative product feedback, and protect revenue generated by popular products.
- **Metric to Track:** Average product rating, percentage of low ratings, number of low-rated reviews, product revenue, and product return rate.

### **Explore Opportunities to Increase Accessory Sales**
- **Findings:** Accessories have the highest average category rating but contribute the least to total revenue. Belts have the highest individual product rating at 3.93.
- **Action:** Introduce cross-selling opportunities by recommending accessories alongside popular clothing and footwear products. Test product bundles, complementary product recommendations, and targeted promotions.
- **Expected Impact:** Increase accessory sales and average transaction value while building on products that already receive positive customer feedback.
- **Metrics to Track:** Accessory revenue, accessory revenue growth, average purchase amount, accessory sales volume, and percentage of transactions containing accessories.

### **Develop Targeted Strategies for Middle-Aged Non-Subscribers** 
- **Findings:** Middle-aged non-subscribers generate the most revenue and leave the most reviews, while maintaining a high average rating.
- **Action:** Investigate the products and categories this segment purchases most frequently. Use personalized recommendations, targeted campaigns, and relevant subscription incentives to encourage continued engagement.
- **Expected Impact:** Maintain revenue from an important customer segment and explore opportunities to increase customer engagement and subscription participation.
- **Metrics to Track:** Revenue by customer segment, average purchase amount, subscription conversion rate, average review rating, and customer engagement.

## **Overall Summary and Business Recommendations** 

The analysis examined the company's sales performance, customer behaviour, and customer satisfaction over the three-year period from January 2022 to December 2024. Overall, the company generated approximately $5.3 million in revenue, with sales increasing by 19% in 2023 and 25% in 2024. Clothing was the strongest-performing category, accounting for approximately 67% of total revenue, with sweaters alone contributing around 43%.

The analysis also identified clear seasonal sales patterns, with Q4 consistently outperforming other quarters and Q1 generating the lowest revenue. Approximately 92% of customer records were classified as not churned, although young adults had a higher churn rate than other age groups. Subscription status did not appear to have a meaningful relationship with average purchase amounts or customer ratings. Additionally, middle-aged non-subscribers represented an important customer segment, generating the highest total revenue.

In terms of customer satisfaction, average review ratings were generally positive. However, some products presented opportunities for improvement. Sweaters generated the highest revenue but also had a substantial number of low reviews, while sandals had the highest percentage of low ratings. Accessories received the highest average category rating despite contributing the least to total revenue.

Based on these findings, the company should consider the following recommendations:

- **Improve product satisfaction:** Investigate customer feedback for sweaters and sandals to identify potential quality or product-related issues. Improving customer satisfaction could help protect revenue from popular products and strengthen the company's reputation.

- **Strengthen seasonal sales strategies:** Prepare for increased Q4 demand through effective inventory planning, marketing, and staffing. Introduce targeted promotions and campaigns during Q1 to improve sales during slower periods.

- **Diversify revenue across product categories:** Explore cross-selling and product bundling opportunities to increase accessory, footwear, and outerwear sales while reducing reliance on clothing.

- **Improve customer engagement:** Develop targeted strategies for young adults, who have the highest churn rate, and investigate opportunities to improve the value of the subscription program.

- **Focus on valuable customer segments:** Better understand the purchasing preferences of middle-aged customers and explore opportunities to maintain engagement and increase customer value.

Overall, the findings demonstrate how data analysis can help identify business strengths, uncover potential areas of improvement, and support more informed decision-making. By balancing sales growth with customer satisfaction and engagement, the company can identify opportunities to strengthen its overall business performance.
